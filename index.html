Analysis interpretation

The histogram shows equal-as-possible mathematical bins, not observed scalar frequency. Partition imbalance is at most one scalar position for balanced intervals. Worker load is an exact sum of interval sizes assigned by the round-robin policy.
No histogram can reveal a hidden pattern in private keys. The domain is a finite integer set; visual bars represent exact counts, not elliptic-curve point clustering or a shortcut to solving the discrete logarithm problem.

Trust and scope

SHA-256 checksums detect accidental changes; they are not signatures. Logical workers do not execute jobs. Manual completion marks are metadata only. This project does not enumerate the scalar domain or perform private-key recovery.
The mathematical domain is represented exactly with integer arithmetic. All displayed percentages are for presentation; source counts and interval endpoints remain exact integers.
